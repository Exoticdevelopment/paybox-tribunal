How it works

Every charge has to earn the right to settle.

01

Promise

The seller writes guarantees in plain language. Tribunal compiles them into typed, versioned checks and says what it can't guarantee.

02

Pay, held

The agent signs an x402 payment through PayBox with passkey approval. The signature is verified, not settled.

03

Judge

Schema, objective rules and grounding checks run first. An AI judge looks only at what rules can't see, and abstains when unsure.

04

Settle or not

Pass: payment settles. Fail: HTTP 422, the payment never moves. Either way, a signed certificate records what happened.

How we keep it honest

  • Every rejected payment is audited on-chain. A seller who settles it outside Tribunal is caught, flagged publicly and drops to a trust score of 0.
  • Objective rules decide first. The AI judge can only reject when two independent reviews agree.
